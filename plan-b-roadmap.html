Week 1: marking, digging, foundation concrete

Days 1–6 · 3 mistri days · 29 mazdoor days · Rs 59,700 wages

  1. Day1

    Demarcation (nishan dehi) Check

    Mark all wall centre lines with rope and chuna from the drawing. Check every room corner is square (equal diagonals).

    Mistri × 1Mazdoor × 2Engineer visit

    On site: Chuna, rope, wooden pegs, measuring tape. Round 1 material starts arriving.

  2. Day2

    Digging (khudai)

    Dig trenches: 2′-6″ wide × 3′-6″ deep for house walls, 2′-0″ × 2′-6″ for parda walls, 1′-6″ × 2′ for left boundary, 2′ × 2′-6″ for front boundary.

    Mazdoor × 7

    On site: Shovels, tasla. Keep dug mitti on the plot.

  3. Day3

    Digging

    Continue trenches.

    Mazdoor × 5
  4. Day4

    Finish digging + trench bottom

    Finish digging. Water and ram trench bottoms. Spray anti-termite. Lay 3″ Ravi rait bed.

    Mazdoor × 5

    On site: Anti-termite, ~190 cft Ravi rait

  5. Day5

    PCC (foundation concrete) Check

    6″ PCC 1:4:8 in all trenches. Level the top.

    Mistri × 1Mazdoor × 5Mixer (optional)

    On site: ~37 bags cement, ~184 cft Chenab rait, ~368 cft bajri

  6. Day6

    PCC finish

    Finish PCC. Keep it wet.

    Mistri × 1Mazdoor × 5

Days are working days. Rain, late material or a short crew will push everything back. Always finish the "Check" step before the next day's work.
