Plan C on the plot. The house sits where the drawing puts it, 16′-3″ back from the front wall. The rear ~62′ stays open for the rear part later.
Three things to tell the engineer

1. Removed wall: taking out the drawing room's left wall makes Kitchen + Entry + Living one 21′-wide space. A 9″×15″ beam (3 + 3 bars of 6 sutar) in the slab, on the old wall line, keeps every slab panel near 14′.
2. No stair: with the stair gone, a future upper floor needs a new stair, for example an outside steel stair in the passage or one in the rear part. Decide before the slab, because a stair opening cannot be cut later.
3. Room door: the 8′-6″ opening is closed, so the Room's door is the old D1 from the kitchen hall.

Crew: 2 mistri + 3 mazdoor, living on site. This is the right size for this job. Add extra daily mazdoors only on digging, PCC, slab-pour and floor days, and a 3rd mistri only for plaster week. With 3–4 mistri and 3–4 mazdoor the brickwork finishes a few days sooner, but every curing wait leaves 7–8 people sitting, and wages go over Rs 5 lakh. Every wait is filled with work that does not depend on it: boundary footings during DPC curing, the front wall while lintels set and while the steel is tied, and the left wall, plaster and parapet during slab curing. Only 6 days in slab curing have nothing left to do, so the crew goes home (chutti) and 1 mazdoor waters the roof.
